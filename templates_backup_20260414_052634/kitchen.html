Technique — what matters
The emulsion is the dish. Rendered guanciale fat carries the flavour. The egg-cheese mixture must never exceed 65°C — above this it scrambles. Off-heat, residual pasta heat completes the sauce.

Starchy pasta water is the only liquid allowed. It controls viscosity. Start with 2–3 tbsp; the consistency should coat the tines of a fork without pooling.

No cream. No onion. No garlic. These are not variations — they are different dishes. The Roman record is clear on this from the 1940s forward.
