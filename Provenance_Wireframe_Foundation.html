About this foundation

This wireframe foundation locks the design system for every subsequent surface wireframe. The aesthetic — warm cream, charcoal, single gold accent, Playfair Display + DM Sans + DM Mono — is the platform's institutional voice rendered visually. Each future wireframe drops into this same file as a new section.

What target state means: the platform shown here assumes Sprints 1 through 7 of the Combined Fix List v2 have shipped. Tier gates render correctly, Cook Mode and Menu Builder ship as features (not placeholders), l'Atelier is the menu engine and Compose Recipe is the per-recipe generator, /cuisines is the curated canon hierarchy, the Founding Partner is editorially placed, and the institutional voice replaces every SaaS phrasing on the public surfaces.

First wireframe: Homepage — open it via the link in the Public Surfaces table above. Next surfaces queued: /pricing, /kitchen, /recipe/<slug>, Cook Mode, Menu Builder, /atelier, /compose, the Founding Partner /suppliers surface.
