## How this manifest works

Every physical and digital cookbook in the founder's collection, organised by primary canon mapping. Format column indicates how the book is held. Source_photo references the photo(s) where the book was identified.

### Format key

- **HC** — Hardcopy (physical book)
- **PDF** — Digital PDF
- **K** — Kindle
- **HC-FR** — Hardcopy, French-language edition (Coquitlam French-language collection)
