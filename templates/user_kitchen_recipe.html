Garth Greenlees
Editor / Producer / Chef
▾

Red Seal Chef, apprenticeship completed in 1988. More than thirty-five years in professional kitchens across Australia, New Zealand, and Canada, including stints as executive chef and owner. Coffee roaster.

His wine education has been done on the ground rather than in a classroom — every major New Zealand wine region (Marlborough, Central Otago, Hawke's Bay, Martinborough, Waiheke, Waipara, Gisborne, Nelson), serious time in Margaret River and elsewhere across Australia, and Italian study through Sardinia, Tuscany, Le Marche, and Emilia-Romagna with close attention to Chianti, Super Tuscans, Vin Santo, Lambrusco, and the traditional producers of Modena and Parma. Provence and the South of France filled in the rest.

The cooking knowledge follows the same pattern: Italy, the South of France, San Diego, New York, Southeast Asia, Sri Lanka, Indonesia, the Yucatán, Timor-Leste, the Pacific Islands, and the Malacca Strait — all places he has lived in or worked through, not read about.

He keeps a personal library of more than 350 cookbooks. He edits Provenance's Sommelier Training Canon, and the structure and curation of its 3,600+ beverage entries are his work. Every recipe published through Provenance is tested in his kitchen before release.
