What Provenance Is

Provenance is a working reference for serious cooks — professional kitchens, home gourmands, and sommeliers alike. Not a recipe app. Not a shortcut. It connects technique to its origin, to the ingredients that make it possible and the people who supply them, at the depth a professional examination demands.

The Standard

Everything in Provenance is written to a single benchmark: the Sashimi Standard. Origin. Quality hierarchy. Sensory identification. Cross-cuisine parallels. The moment where the dish lives or dies. If it cannot meet this standard, it does not exist in Provenance.
