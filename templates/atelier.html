Autumn Kaiseki · Vancouver
6 courses · Pacific Northwest · September
I · Arrival
Dungeness Crab Dashi, Chrysanthemum Snow
Crab from Organic Ocean simmered at 85°C with kombu eight minutes. Freeze-dried chrysanthemum dissolves on contact.
II · Seasonal
Cold-Start Sea Scallop, Celery Root Silk
Taylor Shellfish scallop, cold-start in dry steel. Celery root from North Arm Farm, Pemberton, passed through fine-mesh tamis.
III · Earth
Wild Mushroom Broth, Pine Oil, Cured Yolk
Foraged blend from Mikuni Wild Harvest — chanterelles, matsutake, black trumpet. Egg yolk salt-cured 72 hours.
IV · Protein
Ikejime Spring Salmon, Sansho Butter
Wild chinook from Organic Ocean, dispatched ikejime. Where the dish lives or dies: the moment skin stops pulling from the pan.
V · Pause
Salt Spring Sheep's Cheese, Wild Honeycomb
Farm House Natural Cheeses, sixty-plus days. Honeycomb fractured, not sliced.
VI · Sweet
Quince Tarte Tatin, Nigori Ice
Local quince poached before the tarte is built. Nigori sake churned into a coarse ice — pale opacity reads as snow on the plate.
