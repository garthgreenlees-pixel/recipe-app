Common questions
Can I try it free?
Yes. The first four pillars of every technique — origin, description, cross-cuisine parallels, and flavour context — are always free. Create an account and start exploring immediately.
What's a "pillar"?
Each technique entry is structured around seven pillars: origin, technique description, cross-cuisine parallels, flavour context, quality hierarchy, sensory tests, and species precision. Free access covers the first four. The Kitchen opens all seven.
What payment methods do you accept?
All major credit and debit cards via Stripe. Payments are processed securely — your card details never touch our servers.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Manage your subscription from your account page. You keep access until the end of your billing period.
Is this right for professional kitchens?
The Profession tier is built for working kitchens — programme builder, supplier sourcing, costing tools. The Trade tier adds a self-service portal for producers and suppliers who want to list products.
