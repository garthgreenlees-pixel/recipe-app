Provenance Originals

We're going to the source.

The Commitment

The deepest culinary knowledge does not live in textbooks. It lives in the hands of the people who have made the same dish, in the same place, for generations. Provenance Originals is a field research project to document that knowledge before it disappears.

Twenty-four culinary traditions. Every continent. We go to the source — the home kitchen in Oaxaca, the teff farm in the Ethiopian highlands, the izakaya in Osaka that has been open since 1947 — and we document with the rigour of a professional examination and the patience of a long meal.

Twenty-Four Canons

Each tradition is documented as a Canon: a complete record of origin, technique, ingredient hierarchy, seasonal rhythm, and the cultural context that makes the food legible. The twenty-four traditions span every inhabited continent and represent the foundational pillars of the world's culinary inheritance.

The Table

Each Canon documents six layers of culinary truth. The ingredient hierarchy — what makes one chilli pepper the correct one, and every other a substitute. The technique sequence — the order of operations — where the dish lives or dies. The sensory benchmarks — what the dish smells like when it is right, and what it smells like when it is about to go wrong. The cultural frame — the meal context, the occasion, the table it was built for. The professional standard — what separates a competent execution from a memorable one. And the provenance record — a verified chain of sources from primary producers to living practitioners.

This is not journalism. It is not tourism. It is culinary archaeology conducted with professional standards and published for professional kitchens.
