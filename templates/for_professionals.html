What Makes Provenance Different

Most culinary reference tools are built for home cooks. Provenance is built to the Sashimi Standard: every technique includes origin, quality hierarchy, sensory identification, cross-cuisine parallels, and the precise moment where execution lives or dies.

This isn't crowdsourced. It isn't SEO content. It is a professional knowledge system checked against the standards of international culinary examination bodies.
