What Makes Provenance Different

Most culinary reference tools are built for home cooks. Provenance is built to the Sashimi Standard: every entry includes origin, quality hierarchy, sensory identification, cross-cuisine parallels, and the precise moment where execution lives or dies.

This isn't crowdsourced. It isn't SEO content. It is a professional knowledge system built by a professional chef with 30 years of kitchen experience — and checked against the standards of international culinary examination bodies.
