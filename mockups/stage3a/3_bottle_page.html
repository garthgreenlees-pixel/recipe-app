Oolong-Mother Kombucha

A North Shore kombucha house — North Vancouver, British Columbia · sample producer
750 ml · bottle-conditioned · the doctrine's own example, page one
Free to every reader — origin · description · the region story

Brewed over the bridge in North Vancouver on a high-mountain oolong base, bottle-conditioned until the carbonation is champagne-fine. The mother culture is eleven years old and has never left the North Shore. Dry for the style — the tea leads, the acid follows, sweetness barely speaks.

The North Shore's fermentation habit is mountain-town practical: small tanks, cold rooms cut into hillsides, a farmers'-market counter every Wednesday. This bottle is that habit, poured.
