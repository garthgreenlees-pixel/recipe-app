I  ·  Arrival From the Library
Dungeness Crab Dashi, Chrysanthemum Snow

Crab bodies from Organic Ocean, Vancouver, simmered eight minutes at 85°C with kombu from the Kelp Queen. The extraction window is narrow — pull at pale amber or the brine note dominates. Freeze-dried chrysanthemum from Fresh As dissolves on contact with hot dashi, releasing a faint floral bitterness that cuts the ocean sweetness. Serve in a handmade dashi bowl; the colour of the broth against pale ceramic is where the dish lives or dies.

Technique Dashi extraction · kombu Canon: Japanese
Ingredients Organic Ocean · Vancouver BC origin Fresh As NZ · freeze-dried provider Kelp Queen · Ucluelet BC origin

II  ·  Seasonal From the Library
Cold-Start Sea Scallop, Celery Root Silk, Yuzu Kosho

Single sea scallop from Taylor Shellfish, Puget Sound, placed cold-side down in a dry carbon-steel pan over medium heat. The colour change at the base — translucent up to the midpoint — is the flip signal. Celery root from North Arm Farm, Pemberton, roasted at 200°C until caramelised, then passed through a fine-mesh tamis with cold butter. The yuzu kosho is the heat register: use enough to cut the celery-root sweetness, not so much it overwrites the scallop.

Technique Cold-start sear · dry steel Canon: French technique
Ingredients Taylor Shellfish · Puget Sound WA origin North Arm Farm · Pemberton BC origin

III  ·  Earth Compose draft
Wild Mushroom Broth, Pine Oil, 72-Hour Cured Egg Yolk

A foraged blend from Mikuni Wild Harvest — chanterelles, matsutake fragment, and black trumpet — simmered in the residual dashi base and reduced by half. Cold-pressed pine oil from a BC producer laced in at the moment of service: the oil breaks on contact with the hot broth surface and releases the resinous note before the guest lifts the bowl. Egg yolk slow-cured 72 hours in a 4:1 salt-to-sugar ratio until it holds a wax-like consistency that grates cleanly tableside.

Technique Salt-sugar curing · yolk Forage-led broth reduction
Ingredients Mikuni Wild Harvest · BC / PNW origin

IV  ·  Protein From the Library
Ikejime Spring Chinook, Sansho Butter, Coastal Herb Oil

Wild spring chinook from Organic Ocean, Vancouver, dispatched ikejime. The neurosystem cut prevents lactic acid build-up and extends the window of peak flesh texture to four days — order accordingly. Sear skin-side down in clarified butter for the first three minutes over medium-high heat. Where the dish lives or dies: the moment the skin stops pulling from the pan surface is the moment to flip. Sansho butter mounted cold at the pass; herb oil pressed from coastal forage drizzled last.

Technique Ikejime dispatch Canon: Japanese Pan-sear · skin side
Ingredients Organic Ocean · Vancouver BC origin
Beverage Junmai Ginjo sake pairing Granville Island Brewing (non-alcoholic alt) provider · Vancouver BC

V  ·  Vegan · Pause Compose draft
Roasted Camas Bulb, Smoked Douglas Fir Honey, Pickled Fiddlehead

Camas bulb from Wildest Foods, foraged and sourced with First Nations harvesting protocols. Roasted in the coals of the wood oven until the outer skins are charred and the interior is sweet and dense — a flavour that reads as baked chestnut rather than any onion note. Douglas fir honey cold-smoked for thirty minutes before service. Fiddleheads from North Arm Farm, blanched and quick-pickled in rice vinegar, provide the brightness that the bulb and honey alone cannot supply.

Technique Ember roasting Cold-smoke · honey
Ingredients Wildest Foods · BC forage origin North Arm Farm · Pemberton BC provider

VI  ·  Sweet From the Library
Quince Tarte Tatin, Dark Caramel, Nigori Ice

Local quince — firm enough to resist collapse — poached forty minutes in a light sugar syrup before the tarte is built. The fruit will not soften in the oven alone; the pre-poach is where the texture is set. Caramel taken three degrees past the standard amber to a dark mahogany so the bitterness registers against the sweetness of the quince. Nigori sake from the sake pairing program churned into a coarse, granular ice. The pale opacity of unfiltered sake makes it read as light snow against the dark caramel.

Technique Tarte Tatin inversion Canon: French pastry Dark caramel · mahogany
Beverage Nigori Junmai sake · late-season pairing

Badge wording: "From the Library" = canon-anchored (course pulled from a technique_reference with canon_tier; founder-reviewed; Sashimi Standard certified). "Compose draft" = free-form slot (Mode C) or a slot where the canon pool produced a novel invention; beta operating mode; whimsy; not yet founder-reviewed. Never "AI-generated", never "generated."
Name weight: Canon-anchored course names render in regular weight Playfair (font-weight 500). Free-form course names render in italic Playfair (font-style: italic) — a quiet visual signal of the source, not an explicit label.
Pat's Rule: Every ingredient chip shows origin producer + location. Every beverage chip shows producer or style. Local provider shows when known. The pat-rule span is a secondary label within each chip, not a separate column.
Re-spin: Calls _atelier_compose_slot() for that slot index only, replaces card in place. Does not re-parse the brief or re-run the full composition.

l'Atelier · Badge reference

What the badges mean.

Two badges appear on composition course cards and in the Menu Builder dish rows after a composition is saved. They tell you the provenance of the course — where the structure came from and what review it has received.

From the Library
From the Library
This course was drawn from a canon entry in the technique library — a technique reference with a canon_tier and course_slots defined. The structure, the lineage, and the named producers in the lineage chips were pulled from verified Provenance data — not composed from the brief alone.
Applies when
  • Composition ran in Mode A (explicit canon_entry_id) or Mode B (matched canon via rules engine)
  • The slot was filled from real technique_references and ingredient_products pools
  • The canon entry has been founder-reviewed and is certified at the Sashimi Standard
Compose draft
Compose draft
This course was composed from the brief in Mode C (free-form) or arrived at a course that had no canon pool match. Whimsy is the operating mode. The description, lineage, and structure are plausible but have not yet been founder-reviewed. Safe to save and refine in your Menu Builder before service — treat it as a strong first draft, not a finished plate.
Applies when
  • Brief produced no canon match — Haiku synthesised slot structure from scratch
  • A canon-matched slot produced an invention that could not be validated against real pool IDs
  • Beta operating mode — founder review has not yet run on this course type
Where badges render: On the course card in Screen 4 (top-right). In the Menu Builder dish row (below the dish title, above the remove button). Not shown to diners — only to the member composing or editing the menu.
Beta signal: During the Sprint 9 beta, all Compose draft courses carry a secondary tooltip on hover: "Founder review pending — verify before service." This tooltip is not part of the permanent badge design and will be removed once the review pipeline is running.
Never use: "AI", "generated", "auto-generated", "machine-written", or any phrase that attributes authorship to a model. The badge is about review status, not origin.
