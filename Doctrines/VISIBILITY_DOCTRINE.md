# THE PROVENANCE VISIBILITY DOCTRINE

**Version 1.0 — 5 July 2026 — founder-ruled, platform-wide**
**Durable rule. Lives in `Doctrines/` beside TWO_WATCHER_DOCTRINE.md.**

> "Local by default, global by discovery; the gap is the signal."

This is PLATFORM DOCTRINE, not beverage doctrine. The Beverage Experience
(Stage 3a/3b) is its proving ground, not its boundary. It later extends to
ingredients and the whole Pantry.

---

## 1. LOCAL BY DEFAULT

Every product and producer shows the reader their own region's answer first.
The North Vancouver kombucha surfaces for a North Vancouver reader with its
local provider, per Pat's Rule — ORIGIN earns the citation, PROVIDER earns
the click.

## 2. GLOBAL BY DISCOVERY

That same kombucha, the Carolina iced tea, the Niigata nigori — every product
is discoverable from every region, always. Region filters guide; they never
wall anything off. A curious reader in Tokyo can stand in British Columbia's
cellar and look around.

## 3. THE GAP IS THE SIGNAL

When a reader views or searches a product with NO local provider in their
region, that is an import/export demand signal and it gets recorded — a
lightweight demand ledger:

    product · producer origin · reader region · local-provider-absent · timestamp

No personal data beyond region. This ledger is a foundational Trade-tier
asset and feeds the platform's producer-to-new-market connection business.

## 4. HONEST RENDERING

Where no local provider exists, the page renders origin-only with plain,
confident copy — the product exists, here is where it comes from, it is not
yet carried in your region. An invitation, never an apology. Origin-only
rendering is the doctrine working.

---

## Scope discipline (Stage 3)

Stage 3 builds the visibility rules and the signal capture — NOT a
marketplace, NOT supplier messaging, NOT commerce. The ledger accumulates;
the business acts on it later.

## Spec obligations (Stage 3a)

The Beverage Experience spec must encode this doctrine end to end: the
region door, producer pages, the pairing grammar's suggestions (a suggestion
may name a bottle not locally carried; the rendering rules above apply),
the demand ledger's shape, and where its events fire. The spec must also
name where the doctrine later extends to ingredients and the whole Pantry.

**END DOCTRINE.**
