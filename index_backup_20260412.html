Spaghetti Carbonara
Ingredients
200g spaghetti
100g pancetta
2 eggs
50g Pecorino Romano
Black pepper
Method
1. Cook pasta in salted water until done.
2. Fry pancetta until crispy.
3. Mix eggs and cheese together.
4. Combine everything. Season.

Spaghetti Carbonara
Rome, Lazio · Il Primo Piatto Romano
Al Dente Cook spaghetti in aggressively salted water. Pull 2 minutes early — it finishes in the pan.
Where the dish lives or dies: temperature at the moment of emulsification. Too hot and you have scrambled eggs.
Render Render guanciale (not pancetta) over medium-low. Fat runs clear, not brown. Reserve all fat.
Emulsify Off heat entirely. Add pasta, toss. Add egg-Pecorino mixture slowly, loosening with pasta water.
HACCP Frascati Superiore Lazio, Italy
